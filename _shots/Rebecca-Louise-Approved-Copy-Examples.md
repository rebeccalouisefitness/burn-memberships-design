## Written by Rebecca herself (15 Oct 2026) — BURN app, Nourish › Supplements › How it works

> "I'd keep it exactly this simple."

- **Eyebrow:** HOW IT WORKS

- **Intro:** All you need to do is place your first order, and we'll help you with the rest.
- **1. Place your first order** — Choose the products that are right for you. There's no minimum order to get started!
- **2. Let's get you set up** — We'll jump on a call together and walk you through your products, how to use them, and how to get the best results.
- **3. Unlock your nutrition tools** — Get access to food logging, personalised meal plans, exclusive challenges, one-on-one coaching, and 24/7 AI support — all inside the app!
