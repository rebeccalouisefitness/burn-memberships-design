**WRITING RULE (paste at the start of every copy task):**
Rebecca speaks naturally, warmly, confidently and directly — conversational, simple, encouraging. NOT a marketing agency, corporate site or motivational AI. No clever headlines, unnecessary adjectives, generic inspirational phrases or polished marketing language. Write as if Rebecca is explaining it to a woman sitting next to her. Short, clear, welcoming headlines; practical descriptions of what the user can actually do. Clarity over creativity. If it sounds like an ad, rewrite it. If she wouldn't say it out loud, don't use it. Professional and intelligent, not childish.

**I / we / neither:** "I" only when Rebecca is personally speaking ("I'm so excited to share this with you!"). "We" for the team/coaches/company ("We'll help you get started", "We'll walk you through everything"). Plain direct instructions for app features ("Track your food", "Message your coach"). NEVER imply Rebecca personally delivers coaching/onboarding when a coach/team member does.

**Examples (❌ → ✅):**
- Embark on your wellness journey → Let's get you started!
- Your personalised wellness experience awaits → Everything you need to get started is right here.
- Unlock powerful nutrition tracking → Track your food, hit your protein goals, and see your progress.
- Leverage AI-powered coaching → Need some help? Your AI coach is here for you 24/7.
- Receive expert guidance → You've got a coach here to help you every step of the way.
- Unlock your full potential → You're capable of so much more than you think.
- Progress begins outside your comfort zone → You don't have to get it perfect. You just have to get started.
- Sculpt your dream physique → Let's get stronger, feel fitter, and build that confidence!
- Elevate your fitness routine → Ready for today's workout?
- Order one thing. The rest opens up. → Let's get you started! All you need to do is place your first order, and we'll help you with the rest.

**Headlines:** simple, exciting, instantly clear ("Let's get you started!", "Let's make nutrition simple", "Ready to feel stronger?", "Small changes make a big difference"). Not every headline needs "Let's" or "!" — often plain is best: "Your workouts", "Track your food", "Your meal plan", "Chat with your coach", "See your progress", "Today's workout".

**App instructions:** say what to do next, no paragraphs. E.g. "Keep track of what you're eating, hit your protein goals, and stay on top of your nutrition." [Log My Food] · "Tell us your goals, how many days you want to work out, and how much time you've got. We'll create a plan that works for you!" [Create My Plan] · "Have a question or need a little extra support? Your coach is just a message away." [Message My Coach] · "Ready for a challenge? Join us, stay consistent, and let's see what you can achieve!" [Join the Challenge] · "See how far you've come! Keep track of your workouts, measurements, and progress all in one place." [View My Progress] · "Got a question? Your AI coach is here 24/7 to help with workouts, nutrition, and keeping you on track." [Ask My AI Coach]

**Avoid → use:** embark→get started · journey→progress/goals · empower→help/support · leverage/utilise→use · optimise→improve · elevate→improve/build · seamless→easy/simple · holistic→complete/all-in-one · ecosystem→app/community · transformative→helpful/effective · revolutionary→new · curated→chosen/picked · facilitate→help · unlock your potential→see what you can do · experience→try/use/get · comprehensive suite/robust features→tools/everything you need.

**Process:** for copy work, offer up to 3 options in this voice and don't change app text until Rebecca approves. Keep writing decisions separate from build decisions.
