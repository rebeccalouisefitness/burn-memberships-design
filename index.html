Questions

How do I get into the app after I pay?

You'll land on a thank-you page and get an email from me straight away. Download BURN from the App Store or Google Play, then sign in with the same email you used at checkout. Your membership will be waiting.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I change membership later?

Yes. Move up or down whenever you like.

I already pay through the App Store. Do I need this?

No. If you subscribe in the app, you're all set. Please don't subscribe twice.

Is this the same BURN I've always had?

Same me, same workouts, in a brand-new app. Existing members keep their price.

Notes for Rebecca: what this design needs from you

  1. Approve the design (or tell me what to change). Nothing is live on rebecca-louise.com. It goes there only after your yes, through the same Kajabi loader as /burn-waitlist.
  2. BURN COMPLETE extra features. The two dashed rows are placeholders. Pregnancy and postpartum, recipes, natural remedies, nutrition foundations and mood tracking come from your earlier decisions already in the app. What else goes in COMPLETE?
  3. Annual prices (proposed). $79.99 / $179.99 / $279.99 (10× monthly, so 2 months free). The exact alternative is $79.90 / $179.90 / $279.90. Not created in Stripe until you say yes.
  4. "In build" labels. Activity stats, achievements, Daily Read and cycle-aware training aren't finished in the app yet. Keep them on the page with a label, or hide them until they ship?
  5. Store links. App Store and Google Play links are pending until BURN is live in each store. The TestFlight block is there only for before launch.
  6. Checkout states shown: default, loading (tap any Join button), and error (tick "Show checkout error" in the dark bar, then tap Join).
  7. Photos are your BURN workout covers (Total Body Intense Sculpt, Body Combat, Ultimate Lift Heavy, Unshakeable Strength, Abs & Cardio Fat Burn). Want different ones? Send me the shots and I'll swap them.
