My story

From Eastbourne to Everest

I grew up in Eastbourne, England, playing every sport going. I was never the most talented, just the hardest working. Behind that, I struggled with anorexia, my skin and depression, and learning to think differently changed my life.

At 22 I moved to California to become a commercial pilot, and got my licence. In 2013 I caught the fitness bug and helped my mum lose weight after years of trying. In 2014 I started my YouTube channel, and I’ve posted every week since.

I launched BURN in 2018 so women everywhere could train with me, and rebuilt it in 2022. The same year, I stood on the summit of Mount Everest.
