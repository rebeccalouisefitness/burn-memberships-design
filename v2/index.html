FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m a beginner. Is BURN for me?

Yes. Every workout is tagged Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, so you can filter to your level and build from there. Monthly challenges give you a day-by-day plan, so you never have to guess what to do next.

Do I need equipment?

No. Plenty of workouts need nothing at all, and you can filter for “no equipment”. If you have dumbbells, bands or ankle weights, there are workouts built around those too.

How long are the workouts?

Most are around 30 to 40 minutes, with shorter sessions for busy days. Filter by the time you have.

What’s the difference between Basic, Plus and Complete?

Basic is the core BURN experience: workouts, monthly challenges and progress. Plus adds Luna Bloom and more ways to plan and stay consistent. Complete is everything, including advanced personalization and tracking. See the full comparison.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Memberships are monthly and you can cancel whenever you like. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Which devices does BURN work on?

BURN is an app for iPhone and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the email you joined with.

How do I get the nutrition features?

Nourish is in the app for every member. Recipes, nutrition foundations and personalized meal planning are part of Complete. confirm tier

I’m already a BURN member. Do I need to join again?

No. You keep the price you signed up at. Download the BURN app and sign in with the email you joined with.
