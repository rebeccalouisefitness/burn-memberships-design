Membership questions

What’s the difference between BURN, BURN+ and BURN COMPLETE?

BURN is the core experience: workouts, monthly challenges, the Daily Quote and your progress. BURN+ adds live classes and scheduling, your own playlists, the Daily Read, meditations, the gratitude journal and activity stats. BURN COMPLETE is everything in BURN+, plus Rebecca’s Luna Bloom pregnancy & postpartum programs (Luna Bloom Pregnancy Workouts and the Luna Bloom 12-Month Postpartum Program), recipes and nutrition foundations, and mood tracking. See the full comparison.

Where are the pregnancy and postpartum workouts?

They’re Rebecca’s Luna Bloom programs, and they come with BURN COMPLETE. Set your life stage (pregnancy or postpartum) in your profile and the app shows you the right week or month.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Memberships are monthly and you can cancel whenever you like. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

Which devices does BURN work on?

BURN is an app for iPhone and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the email you joined with.

I’m already a BURN member. Do I need to join again?

No. You keep the price you signed up at. Download the BURN app and sign in with the email you joined with.
