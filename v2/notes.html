Notes for Rebecca

Design v2, built from your brief. Nothing is live on rebecca-louise.com, nothing was created in Stripe or Kajabi, and no money moves. The older v1 design has been taken down from the preview site.

Hard requirement

A desktop purchase unlocks the app

You said: “Ok and make sure if they sign up on desktop it still gets them access into the app”

  • Account first, then pay. Every Join button now goes to Create your account (first name, email, password, confirm) before checkout. It is the same login the BURN app uses (email + password), so whoever buys on a laptop already has their app account.
  • Checkout is tied to that account. The checkout email is filled in and locked to the account email, and the purchase carries the account’s app id, so RevenueCat puts the membership on exactly that account.
  • Thank-you and Download pages say “Download BURN on your phone and sign in with [their email]”, in 3 steps. On a computer they show a QR code to scan with the phone camera. On a phone the QR is hidden and the store buttons show instead.
  • Release blocker: before launch we buy on a desktop in Stripe test mode, sign in on a phone (TestFlight) with the same email, and check the right tier unlocks, for all three tiers (plan, test T1).

Pages

  • Home: your 11 sections, in your order, with your headlines.
  • Workouts, Memberships (tiers + comparison table), About. How It Works jumps to section 5 on Home.
  • Create your account (new): step 2 of 3, before checkout. Shows the tier and price, has “Already have a BURN app account? Sign in”, and the note “Use this same email to sign in on your phone.” Then a pretend checkout (email locked to the account), then Thank you. Nothing is created or charged.
  • Thank you (“Welcome to BURN [tier]”, the sign-in email, 3 steps, QR on computers, store badges, TestFlight, “Didn’t get access? Contact support”) and Download / open the app (same steps and QR, sign-in reminder, “Paid with a different email?” help).

Click tracking: Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel

Decided (B): both GA4 and the Meta Pixel. On this preview nothing is sent to Google or Meta. The tags only load on rebecca-louise.com, and only once BURN’s own GA4 and Pixel IDs are filled in, so the preview can’t pollute your ad data. Here, every event still goes to the browser console and to this counter. Click any Join Now, scroll to the pricing, or finish a pretend checkout, then come back here.

  • Better still, server-side: registration_complete / purchase should be confirmed from the server (Meta Conversions API, and GA4 through RevenueCat or a Stripe webhook) using the same event_id, so Meta de-duplicates the browser and server copies. The browser alone misses ad-blocked buyers and can double count.
  • Before the tags go live for UK/EU visitors, the site needs a cookie consent banner.
