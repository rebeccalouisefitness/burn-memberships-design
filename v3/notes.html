Decisions I made (easy to change)

  • When a feature is on your list as New but isn’t built (Progress photos), it shows Coming, not New.
  • BURN COMPLETE gets the highlight and the label “Most personalised”.
  • The nutrition add-on has no price and its button says “Ask about nutrition” (an email to customer service). It’s tagged Coming because the in-app plan is only partly built.
  • The checkout step says “Demo checkout: no card is charged.”, so nobody on the preview link thinks they’re paying.
  • Annual saving is “save 33%” for COMPLETE (12 × $27.99 = $335.88, and $224.99 is 33% less).
