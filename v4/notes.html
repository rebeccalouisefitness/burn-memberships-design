What changed in v4

  1. Photos of you: purple outfit only. Hero, Meet Rebecca, the final call to action, About (both photos), the MOVE card and every workout tile now use only your purple-outfit shoot. Every beach, pool, Everest and other-outfit photo is gone from v4, including the old workout and program covers (the library now uses the same category photos as the app’s Move tab). On a 390px phone the hero photo sits behind the headline and Join Now is on the first screen.
  2. Inside the app: real iPhone screenshots. Four real screenshots of the BURN app (Move, your challenge calendar, Nourish, Progress), shown in an iPhone frame. The same screenshots replace the drawn mock-ups on Workouts and Download.
  3. “Your goals. Your schedule. Your plan.” is gone. It’s now “Build your workout. Schedule your week.” (BURN+): pick one or more workouts from the library, pick a day, pick a time or join a live class, and it shows your week. The Workouts page section matches. The quiz now asks whether you want to “build my own workouts and schedule my week” (BURN+) or have “My BURN plan, which I can adjust” (BURN COMPLETE, as on your list).
  4. Move · Nourish · Thrive. MOVE = you in the purple set mid-workout. NOURISH = a food photo (a colourful bowl). THRIVE = a sunrise meditation/mindset photo. “Find Your Perfect Workout” stays, and now says the challenge changes every month.
  5. Annual shows the monthly price. With Annual selected, every price shows the monthly equivalent big with “Billed $X yearly” underneath: BURN $7.00/month (billed $83.99 yearly, save 12%), BURN+ $14.00/month (billed $167.99 yearly, save 22%), BURN COMPLETE $18.75/month (billed $224.99 yearly, save 33%). Worked out in the code as annual ÷ 12, rounded to cents. Monthly still shows $7.99 / $17.99 / $27.99. This applies on Home, Memberships, the comparison table, the quiz result, join, checkout and thank-you. Checkout and thank-you also show the amount actually paid today ($83.99 / $167.99 / $224.99).
  6. All “Coming soon” is gone: tags, legend, FAQ question, quiz, the existing-members line, the nutrition add-on and the store-badge line. Features are listed as they are. The New tags on Favourites, Community chat, Daily quote and Progress photos stay.
  7. Support is customerservice@rebecca-louise.com everywhere. No certifications line anywhere.

Everything else from v3 is the same: the Find your BURN quiz, merged sections, nutrition add-on card, 3 reviews + “Read more reviews”, join → account → demo checkout → thank-you with QR → download, and the click tracking.

App screenshots: where they came from

iOS Simulator, iPhone 16 Pro (iOS 26.5), real device resolution 1206 × 2622, running the BURN build installed on the QA simulator (build 0.1.0 (12)) with today’s update from the update server. Status bar set to 9:41, full battery. Screens: Move (library by body part, your purple photos), Sweat or Treat challenge calendar, Nourish › Recipes, Progress.
