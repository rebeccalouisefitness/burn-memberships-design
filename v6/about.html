My story

From Eastbourne to Everest

I grew up in Eastbourne, England, playing every sport going. I was never the most talented, just the hardest working. Behind that, I struggled with anorexia, my skin and depression, and learning to think differently changed my life.

At 22 I moved to California to become a commercial pilot, and I got my licence. A year later, in 2012, I filmed my first workout video for YouTube. I was 23, I had no idea what I was doing, and I’ve posted every week since. Along the way I helped my mum lose the weight she’d been fighting for years, and that’s when I knew this was what I was meant to do.

I launched BURN in 2018 so women everywhere could train with me, and rebuilt it in 2022. The same year, I stood on the summit of Mount Everest.

Everest taught me the same thing every workout does: you don’t have to feel ready, you just have to take the next step. That’s what I want BURN to be for you.
