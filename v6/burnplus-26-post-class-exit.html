How it works

1 · The moment the class ends

  • Her finish and attendance are saved first, then the pop-up opens. Closing it never undoes anything.
  • It shows only when she actually did the class (90%, your rule). If she left early she gets the #27 "Finish it" path instead, never a celebration.
  • It reuses the parts of the "Workout complete" screen you already have (tick ring, three numbers, the Story and Post share cards and the share sheet), with "Class complete" and the class time on them.
  • Share your class opens the existing share sheet (Instagram Stories, Facebook, save image…). Back to BURN or ✕ skips it.

2 · After sharing (or skipping)

  • She's back in the room while it's still open, with the round back button top left and a Back to BURN button that's always visible. No "Done" anywhere.
  • Both take her to Today, where the day is already ticked (#27, live in Preview 204).
