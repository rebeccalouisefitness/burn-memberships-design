What changes

Added

  • Back button (top left): the same round button the waiting room already has. It goes back to wherever she came from, usually Today.
  • Done (bottom): always visible under the cheers, so she can leave without scrolling. It also goes back to Today.
  • The phone's swipe-back and Android's back button do the same thing.

Kept exactly as is

  • Stats, "The room's still open", the cheers, "Book tomorrow", the emoji bar.
  • Leaving doesn't undo anything: her completed workout, attendance and cheers are all saved first. She can go back into the room from Today while it's still open.

Not in this change

  • The celebration (#25), the recap and the completion tick on Today (#27), and the copy (#24/#25). Each comes later as its own before/after.
