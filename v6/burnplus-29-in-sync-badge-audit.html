BURN+ #29 · The "In Sync" badge

A member got "In Sync" and didn't know what it meant or what she'd done. Looking at how it's counted, it was also given wrongly: it's awarded as soon as there are two period-start dates, even if they're only 8 days apart (for example a period logged on 9 Oct and logged again on 17 Oct). The unlock screen stays the same; it just explains itself, and the badge is only given for a real cycle.

What changes

The rule (the real fix)

  • Today: two period-start dates = "one full cycle", however close together.
  • Proposed: it counts only when two period starts are 21 to 45 days apart, the normal range for a cycle. Logging the same period twice, or an accidental log a week later, no longer counts.
  • Setting up Cycle, one period entry, or an estimated phase never counts. Members who don't track their cycle are never nudged to.

The screen

  • Same unlock screen, same confetti, Share and Keep going.
  • The name says what it is, and a short "What you did" box explains it, with the date it was earned.
  • The bottom line is labelled "Also close:" so it no longer reads as the next step after cycle tracking, and it says "Daily Reads" instead of just "reads".
  • The badge detail sheet (tapping it in the collection) gets the same explanation.

Your OK needed on

  • Name: "My First Cycle", "Cycle Tracker", or keep "In Sync" with the explanation underneath?
  • The "What you did" sentence and the privacy line under it.
  • 21–45 days as "a real cycle"? Perimenopause cycles can be longer. Should perimenopause mode allow up to 90 days?
  • Members who already got it wrongly (two logs close together): take it back quietly, or let them keep it? Your rule so far has been "earned badges are never taken away".
