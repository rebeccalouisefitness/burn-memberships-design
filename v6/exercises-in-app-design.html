Exercises on the workout screen

These are screenshots of the real BURN app (the current workout screen) with the new Exercises section added, using today's challenge workout, Blast Lower Belly Fat & Strengthen Core. The exercise list was drafted automatically from the video itself (what you say plus what's on screen). It's closed by default, as a tidy "Exercises · 15 moves" row, and opens with one tap.

1 · Closed (how everyone sees it)

A single row under Complete: "Exercises · 15 moves · 3 rounds" with a little arrow. Everything else on the screen stays as it is.

2 · Opened

Tap the row to open it. Warm up, Round 1-3 and Cool down, each with "Repeat x3" where the moves repeat. Numbered moves, the set length, and "Each side" where it applies.

3 · While she's working out

Tap any time (e.g. ▶ 9:07) and the video jumps straight to that move. The move playing now is highlighted, and the ones she's done get a tick.
What happens next if you're happy: we draft the list for every workout the same way, check a sample against the videos, and ship it to your app and to the testers. Workouts without a list simply don't show the row. Names can be corrected any time without an app update.
