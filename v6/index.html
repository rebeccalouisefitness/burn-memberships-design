BURN+ and BURN Complete

Train with your cycle

Your energy can change through the month. Tell us a little about your cycle and we’ll match your workouts to it. Every day, the app recommends the workout to do for your phase. Only you can see this.

Rest is training too. Walking, mobility, gentle Pilates and lighter strength. Modify, don’t skip.

Picked for your phase

Always go by how you feel, not the prediction. Irregular, on birth control or in perimenopause? We’ll go by how you feel each day.

BURN’s cycle guidance is general wellness information, not medical advice. Predictions are estimates and are not a form of contraception. If something feels wrong, talk to your doctor.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    I’m a beginner. Is BURN for me?

    Yes. Every workout is marked Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, so you can filter to your level and build from there. Each monthly challenge gives you a plan for every day, so you never have to guess what to do next.

    Do I need equipment?

    No. Plenty of workouts need nothing at all, and you can filter for no equipment. If you have dumbbells, bands or ankle weights, there are workouts built around those too.

    What’s the difference between BURN, BURN+ and BURN Complete?

    BURN is the core experience: 40+ programs, monthly challenges and guides, recipes, the Thrive library, step tracking, favourites, community chat, the daily quote and progress photos. BURN+ adds the tools to make it yours: build your own workouts with your own playlists and schedule your week, live classes, the Daily Read, your gratitude journal, activity stats, progress & achievements and cycle-aware training. BURN Complete is your most personalised BURN experience: My BURN plan, Ask BURN AI and points for the store, Rebecca’s Luna Bloom pregnancy & postpartum programs and every new feature first. See the full comparison.

    Can I build my own workouts and plan my week?

    Yes, with BURN+ and BURN Complete. Put your own workouts together from the library and schedule them on the days and times that suit you, or join a live class. Want a plan built for you? That’s My BURN plan, in BURN Complete. See how it works.

    Is nutrition included?

    Every membership includes recipes with diet filters, and every membership can add the nutrition experience. Once you start a nutrition plan, you unlock food tracking, meal plans and coaching in the app, for free. About nutrition.

    Where are the pregnancy and postpartum workouts?

    Rebecca’s Luna Bloom pregnancy & postpartum programs are part of BURN Complete. Set your life stage in the app and it shows you the right week or month.

    Monthly or annual, and can I cancel any time?

    Choose monthly, or pay once a year and save up to 33%. A year is $83.99 for BURN, $167.99 for BURN+ and $224.99 for BURN Complete. You can cancel whenever you like and keep access until the end of the month or year you’ve paid for.

    Which devices does BURN work on?

    BURN is an app for iPhone and Android. Join here, download the app and sign in with the same email and password.

    I’m already a BURN member. Do I need to join again?

    Yes. This is a brand-new app, so you’ll need to sign up. Keep an eye on your email: we’re sending every current member a special invitation code to use when you join.
