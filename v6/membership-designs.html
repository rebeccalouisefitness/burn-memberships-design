Upgrading a membership in the app

What Profile › Membership shows, and what Upgrade does, depending on where she bought BURN. Nobody is ever billed twice: the app always knows whether her membership came from the website (Stripe), the App Store or Google Play, and only offers the matching upgrade.

A. Bought on the website, upgrades inside the app Recommended (US)

She never leaves BURN. Upgrade opens a secure sheet over the app with the BURN upgrade page. She pays the difference with Apple Pay in one tap, the sheet closes, and BURN COMPLETE is on straight away.

Pro: no app-store fee on the upgrade (Apple takes 0% on US website purchases today) and it stays one membership. Con: US App Store only for now; Apple has asked a court for a fee of up to 15% on these purchases, not yet decided.

B. Bought on the website, outside the US Fallback

Apple doesn’t allow the website sheet in the app outside the US. Here the upgrade is an Apple purchase, and BURN cancels her website membership and refunds the unused days automatically, so she’s never charged twice.

Pro: works in every country, one tap with Face ID. Con: Apple keeps 15–30% of the upgrade, and she moves from a website membership to an App Store one.

Behind the scenes

  • Every upgrade or plan change updates RevenueCat, the app’s plan, and (once email is built) her Kajabi tag: BURN / BURN+ / BURN COMPLETE.
  • Website price changes are worked out to the day by Stripe. Store upgrades are worked out by Apple or Google.
  • Today in the app, “Cancel” always opens App Store subscriptions and an upgrade would start an Apple purchase even for a website buyer. Both are fixed by this design.
  • Example prices assume she upgrades halfway through her month.
