Your membership & upgrading

An upgrade always goes through the same place she first paid, so it changes her existing membership and never starts a second one. The app works out where she bought BURN (website, App Store or Google Play) and which phone she’s on, and shows the right screen automatically.

Joined on the websiteHer Stripe membership is upgraded. One tap, charged to the card she already used, priced to the day.
Bought in the App StoreApple upgrades her Apple subscription. Apple works out the price to the day.
Bought on Google PlayGoogle upgrades her Google Play subscription, priced to the day.

How it works behind the scenes
  • Never two memberships. The app reads where she bought BURN and only shows that upgrade. The App Store/Google Play buttons are never shown to a website member, and the website upgrade is never shown to a store member.
  • Website upgrades change her existing Stripe subscription (same card, same renewal date, priced to the day). The confirm sheet is a secure web page over the app, which Apple allows in the US with no fee today (Apple has asked a court for up to 15%, not yet decided).
  • Website members on iPhone outside the US (Apple doesn’t allow the web sheet there): the app shows her plan without an upgrade button, and upgrades are offered by email instead. Same on Android outside the US.
  • After any change, RevenueCat updates her plan, the app refreshes, and (once emails are built) her Kajabi tag changes to match.
  • Example prices assume she upgrades halfway through her month.
