Behind the scenes (she never sees this)
  • The app knows where she first paid (website, App Store or Google Play) and upgrades that same membership. Never a second one.
  • “Pay the difference” is worked out to the day. Example: $5.00 is halfway through her month.
  • Switching down (COMPLETE → BURN+ → BURN) goes through wherever she first paid, keeps the same membership and starts at the end of the current month or year. Cancel also ends at the end of the period. Save offers are once per member.
  • Card “Change” shows Apple Pay on iPhone, Google Pay on Android.
