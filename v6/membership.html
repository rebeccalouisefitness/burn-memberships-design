Membership questions

I’m a beginner. Is BURN for me?

Yes. Every workout is marked Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, so you can filter to your level and build from there. Each monthly challenge gives you a plan for every day, so you never have to guess what to do next.

Do I need equipment?

No. Plenty of workouts need nothing at all, and you can filter for no equipment. If you have dumbbells, bands or ankle weights, there are workouts built around those too.

What’s the difference between BURN, BURN+ and BURN COMPLETE?

BURN is the core experience: 40+ programs, monthly challenges and guides, recipes, the Thrive library, step tracking, favourites, community chat, the daily quote and progress photos. BURN+ adds live classes, the Daily Read, your gratitude journal, activity stats, progress & achievements and cycle-aware training. BURN COMPLETE is your most personalised BURN experience: build your own workouts with your own playlists and schedule your week, My BURN plan, AI support chat, points for the store, Rebecca’s Luna Bloom pregnancy & postpartum programs and every new feature first. See the full comparison.

Can I build my own workouts and plan my week?

Yes, with BURN COMPLETE. Put your own workouts together from the library and schedule them on the days and times that suit you. Live classes are included from BURN+. See how it works.

Is nutrition included?

Every membership includes recipes with diet filters. Your own nutrition plan, built with a coach, is a separate add-on you can add to any membership. About the nutrition add-on.

Where are the pregnancy and postpartum workouts?

Rebecca’s Luna Bloom pregnancy & postpartum programs are part of BURN COMPLETE. Set your life stage in the app and it shows you the right week or month.

Monthly or annual, and can I cancel any time?

Choose monthly, or pay once a year and save up to 32%. Annual works out at $6.99 a month for BURN (billed $83.99 yearly), $13.99 a month for BURN+ (billed $167.99 yearly) and $18.99 a month for BURN COMPLETE (billed $227.99 yearly). You can cancel whenever you like and keep access until the end of the month or year you’ve paid for.

Which devices does BURN work on?

BURN is an app for iPhone and Android. Join here, download the app and sign in with the same email and password.

I’m already a BURN member. Do I need to join again?

No. You keep the price you signed up at. Download the BURN app and sign in with the email you joined with. New for BURN members: favourites, community chat, daily quote and progress photos.
