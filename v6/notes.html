App screenshots: where they came from

iOS Simulator, iPhone 16 Pro (iOS 26.5), real device resolution 1206 × 2622, running the BURN build installed on the QA simulator (build 0.1.0 (12)) with today’s update from the update server. Status bar set to 9:41, full battery. Screens: Move (library by body part, your purple photos), Sweat or Treat challenge calendar, Nourish › Recipes, Progress. The frame is a clean iPhone 16 Pro-style frame drawn in CSS (sharp at any size), not an Apple marketing frame.

Left out on purpose: the Today screen, because the only signed-in account on the simulator is the App Review test account and Today greets it as “Good afternoon, Reviewer”; Community, because it shows a real tester’s post and “2 members”; Thrive, because of the “Become an ambassador” card; the class-booking screen, because it has no class times yet.
