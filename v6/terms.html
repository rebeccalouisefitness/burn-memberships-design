3. Payment and auto-renewal

When you join on this website, you pay today for your first month or year. Your membership then renews automatically at the same price, every month or every year, on the same date, until you cancel. We’ll charge the card you joined with.

Payments on this website are handled securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

If a payment doesn’t go through, your access may pause until it’s sorted.

If we ever change the price of your membership, we’ll tell you before the new price applies, so you can cancel first if you want to.

4. Cancelling

You can cancel any time, right in the BURN app: go to Subscription and tap Cancel. If you’d rather, you can also email customerservice@rebecca-louise.com with the subject “CANCEL” and the name and email address you joined with.

When you cancel, you won’t be charged again, and you keep your access until the end of the month or year you’ve already paid for.

5. Refunds

Payments aren’t refundable once your month or year has started, and we don’t refund part-used months or years. If you joined twice by mistake, email us and we’ll refund the extra membership. This doesn’t affect any rights you have under the law where you live.
